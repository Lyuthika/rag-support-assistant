\# Creating a page



Source URL: https://www.notion.com/help/guides/creating-a-page



Category: pages-editing



Every new Notion page you create is a blank canvas where you can add content including text, images, to-do lists, and databases.



To create a new page, click on the New page button in your sidebar.



A menu of page types will appear, allowing you to turn your empty page into a table, board, list, or simply keep it as an empty page.



You can give your new page a title, an icon for easy identification later, and some initial content.



Type the forward slash key to see a list of different content types to add to your page. Select the content you'd like to add with your mouse.
